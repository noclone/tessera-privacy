Privacy Policy — Tessera

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Français · English

Tessera is a general-knowledge game: one question a day, with groups of friends and a confidence-betting system. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and what your rights are.

The publisher of Tessera is the controller of your data. For any question or request, write to jeanfechter@gmail.com.

Data we collect

Account. If you create an account with an email, we store your email address, your username and your password (stored encrypted, never in clear text). If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, your display name and your Google account identifier.

Guest use. You can play without an account: an anonymous identifier is then created to keep your progress on the device. If you later create an account, this progress can be linked to your account.

Game data. Your answers to questions, your scores, your confidence bets, your streaks and the associated dates.

Social data. The groups you create or join, your membership in these groups, invitation codes, as well as the challenges you send or receive and their answers.

Content you send us. The questions you propose, error reports and improvement suggestions (free text).

Notifications. If you enable notifications, a device token (provided by Firebase Cloud Messaging) is stored so we can send you push notifications.

Purchases. If you make a purchase in the app (Tessera+, token packs), we keep your purchase history in order to grant you the corresponding features. The payment itself is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store): we never see your card number.

Advertising. The app displays ads provided by Google AdMob. For this purpose, your device's advertising identifier as well as interaction and diagnostic data may be collected and shared with Google to serve and measure ads. On first launch, a consent screen (GDPR) lets you choose between personalized and non-personalized ads. Tessera+ accounts see no ads.

We do not resell any of your data.

Why we use this data

  • Run the game: display the daily question, calculate and keep your scores and streaks.
  • Enable social features: groups, leaderboards and challenges between friends.
  • Process the questions, reports and suggestions you send.
  • Send you notifications (streak reminders, activity in your groups) if you have accepted them.
  • Offer and unlock in-app purchases (Tessera+, token packs).
  • Display ads and measure their performance (except for Tessera+ accounts).
  • Ensure the security and proper functioning of the service.

Legal basis (GDPR)

Processing your account and game data is necessary to provide the service you request. Sending push notifications relies on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in your device settings. Displaying personalized ads also relies on your consent, collected via the consent screen on first launch and changeable at any time.

Who has access to your data

Your data is hosted and processed by our technical providers, acting on our behalf:

  • Supabase — hosting of the database, authentication and backend.
  • Google Firebase — sending push notifications (Cloud Messaging) and Google sign-in.
  • Google AdMob — serving and measuring ads; for this it receives your advertising identifier and usage data.
  • RevenueCat — managing and validating your in-app purchases.
  • Apple / Google Play — processing in-app purchase payments.

These providers may process data outside the European Union. In that case, appropriate safeguards (standard contractual clauses) govern these transfers. Apart from serving ads (Google AdMob), we do not share your data with third parties for commercial purposes, and we do not resell it.

The other members of your groups can see your username, your scores within the group and the challenges you share with them.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account or ask us to, your personal data is deleted, except for what the law would require us to keep.

Your rights

In accordance with the GDPR, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction and objection, as well as the right to withdraw your consent. To exercise these rights or delete your account, write to jeanfechter@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Security

Passwords are stored encrypted and exchanges with the server are protected. As no method is completely secure, we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we implement reasonable measures to protect your data.

Children

Tessera is not intended for children under 15. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

This policy may change. In the event of a significant change, we will inform you in the app. The date of the last update appears at the top of this document.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your data: jeanfechter@gmail.com.
